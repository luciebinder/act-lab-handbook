# Welcome to the ACT-Lab Handbook 👋

This handbook is thought to be your go-to resource for working and conducting research in the **Department of General Psychology II (ACT-Lab)** at Goethe University Frankfurt. Whether you're just starting as a new staff member, a HiWi, or a PhD student — this handbook is here to help you find your footing and get up to speed with how things work in the lab.

## What you'll find here

**Getting Started** — WiFi and VPN, file sharing with Hessenbox, vacation requests and absence notifications.

**Starting your PhD** — Everything you need to know about registering as a doctoral researcher, writing your dissertation, and professional development opportunities through GRADE, IKH, and Mentoring Hessen.

**Conducting Research** — Ethics proposals, open science practices (OSF, LIFOS, pre-registration), data collection with Sona System, and literature management with Zotero.

**Teaching** — Courses offered by the department and teaching resources including pandaR, Virtuelle Lehre, and studiumdigitale.

**Contributing to this Handbook** — How to collaborate, fork, and keep this handbook up to date via GitHub.

Use the **table of contents on the left** to browse by topic. Each section is self-contained, so feel free to jump straight to whatever you need right now.
