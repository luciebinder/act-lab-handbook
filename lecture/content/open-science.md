# Open Science

Open science practices are an integral part of how we conduct and share research in the ACT-Lab. This page provides an overview of the key platforms and practices we use.

---

## Pre-registration

Pre-registration means documenting your research questions, hypotheses, and analysis plan *before* data collection begins. This increases transparency and helps distinguish confirmatory from exploratory analyses.

In our lab, **pre-registration is standard practice** — both for our own research and for student theses. Pre-registrations can be submitted via [OSF](https://osf.io/) or [AsPredicted](https://aspredicted.org/).

### AsPredicted

[AsPredicted](https://aspredicted.org/) guides you through 11 questions about your planned study. A practical approach is to first draft your answers in a Word document, then enter the final version online. You will receive a **timestamped PDF** of your pre-registration.

## LIFOS — Lokale Infrastruktur für Open Science

[LIFOS](https://lifos.uni-frankfurt.de) is a local open science platform developed by the Methods department at the Department of Psychology, Goethe University Frankfurt. It provides a space to practice modern open science methods in the context of coursework.

Students completing their thesis in our lab are required to upload their **pre-registration, materials, code and data** to LIFOS. Files are stored long-term and accessible within the department.
