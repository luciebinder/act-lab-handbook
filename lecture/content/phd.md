# Doing a PhD

This page provides an overview of the key steps involved in completing a PhD at the Institute of Psychology.

## 1. Registration as a Doctoral Researcher

The first step is the formal registration as a doctoral researcher at the department. This includes finding a supervisor, submitting the required documents, and being officially accepted into the PhD programme.

The following documents are required for registration:

- Supervision agreement (*Betreuungsvereinbarung*), including the certificate of completion of the e-learning course "Gute wissenschaftliche Praxis in der Promotion" and a work and timeline plan
- Copy of your Abitur and Master's or Diploma certificate
- Declaration that you have not previously applied for a doctoral examination
- Exposé (2–3 pages) and preliminary working title
- Declaration that the principles of good scientific practice will be observed
- Self-registration in HISinOne

```{seealso}
Full details on the registration process including checklists and all needed documents can be found on the [department website](https://www.psychologie.uni-frankfurt.de/146466893/Das_Annahmeverfahren_als_Doktorand_in).
```

## 2. Writing the Dissertation

When doing a publication-based dissertation, you need to write a **cover paper (*Manteltext*)** of approximately **30 pages** in addition to your compiled publications. This text should provide a critical overview of your work from a broader perspective, including a theoretical introduction to your research questions, a summary of the empirical studies and their findings (comprehensible without reading the individual papers), and an overarching discussion that answers your research questions and highlights the contribution of your work.

For the rules on publication-based dissertations, please refer to the document [Regeln für die Eröffnung und Durchführung des Prüfungsverfahrens](https://www.psychologie.uni-frankfurt.de/167615724/regeln-fuer-die-eroeffnung-und-durchfuehrung-von-pruefungsverfahren-promotionen.pdf).


## 3. Opening the Examination Procedure

Once your dissertation is complete, you need to formally open the examination procedure (*Eröffnung des Prüfungsverfahrens*) to initiate the review and defence process.

```{tip}
As soon as you have a rough idea of when you'd like to submit — or when you start writing your dissertation (i.e., *Manteltext*) — get in touch with the responsible person in the department (see contact information on the right hand side [here](https://www.psychologie.uni-frankfurt.de/142247386/Promotionen_am_Institut_f%C3%BCr_Psychologie)) to find out when the next doctoral committee meetings (*Promotionsausschüsse*) are scheduled. These dates determine your submission deadline and may shape your entire timeline.
```

```{seealso}
More information can be found on the [department website](https://www.psychologie.uni-frankfurt.de/146471215/Er%C3%B6ffnung_des_Pr%C3%BCfungsverfahrens).
```
