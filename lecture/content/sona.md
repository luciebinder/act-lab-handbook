# Sona System

[Sona System](https://www.psychologie.uni-frankfurt.de/157444303/VPM) is the platform used by psychology students at Goethe University to collect their required research participation hours (*Versuchspersonenminuten*, VPM). As a researcher, you can upload your studies to Sona to recruit participants.

Sona is relevant in several contexts:

- **Your own research projects**
- **Thesis supervision** — students may recruit participants via Sona for their theses
- **EXPRA** — students conduct experiments and use Sona for recruitment
