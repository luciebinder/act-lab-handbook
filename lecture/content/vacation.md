# Vacation & Absence

## Submitting a Vacation Request

Vacation requests are submitted via the **university portal** [https://fiori.hehrz.de/fiori](https://fiori.hehrz.de/fiori).

```{tip}
Try to plan your vacation during **lecture-free periods** where possible. Otherwise, check with the head of lab.
```


**Step-by-step:**

1. Go to the portal and select **"Goethe Universität Frankfurt"**, then log in with your **HRZ login**.

```{important}
The university portal is only accessible via the **university network** or **VPN client**. If you are working from home, make sure to connect to the VPN first.
```

2. Navigate to **Personal → Abwesenheitsantrag**.

![Image of the fiona website](../static/vacation_personal.png)

3. At the top, you'll find an overview of your total vacation days, how many have been used, and how many are remaining. Below that, you can see your previous and pending requests and their status.

![Overview of Abwesenheitsantrag site.](../static/vacation_overview.pdf)


4. To submit a new request, click **"Abwesenheitsantrag anlegen"** in the lower right corner.

![Overview of how a request looks like.](../static/vacation_request.pdf)

5. Set your start and end date and click **"Sichern"**. The request will automatically be forwarded to Sabine for approval.


```{note}
Christmas Eve (Dec 24) and New Year's Eve (Dec 31) each count as only **half a vacation day**.
```
