# Teaching Resources

## Lehre virtuell

[Lehre virtuell](https://lehre-virtuell.uni-frankfurt.de/) is the central knowledge portal for digital teaching at Goethe University. It provides information, tutorials, and guidance on designing digital courses.

The portal covers a range of topics and tools, as shown in the overview below.

[![Overview of Virtuelle Lehre](../static/lehre-virtuell.png)](https://lehre-virtuell.uni-frankfurt.de/)

## studiumdigitale

[studiumdigitale](https://www.studiumdigitale.uni-frankfurt.de/) is the central innovation unit for technology-enhanced teaching and learning at Goethe University. They develop digital tools and approaches to improve teaching, and offer a range of services for instructors.

They provide instructions and support for tools such as [Moodle](https://www.studiumdigitale.uni-frankfurt.de/lehre/tools/moodle), [AI-ToolLab](https://www.studiumdigitale.uni-frankfurt.de/lehre/tools/ai-toollab), [LernBar](https://www.studiumdigitale.uni-frankfurt.de/lehre/tools/lernbar), [HyperChalk](https://www.studiumdigitale.uni-frankfurt.de/lehre/tools/hyperchalk), and [Etherpad](https://www.studiumdigitale.uni-frankfurt.de/lehre/tools/etherpad).

## IKH — Interdisziplinäres Kolleg Hochschuldidaktik

The [IKH](https://www.ikh.uni-frankfurt.de/) supports the professionalisation of teaching at Goethe University. They offer workshops and training in higher education didactics — particularly relevant if you are involved in teaching as a **PhD student or staff member**.

Their work focuses on three areas: supporting and improving teaching, fostering networking among instructors, and conducting pedagogical research.

Browse the full [workshop programme](https://www.psychologie.uni-frankfurt.de/117269418/Workshopangebot) for upcoming offerings. Examples include:

- Stimme und Körpersprache in der Lehre
- Strategische Planung von Lehrveranstaltungen
- Moderation von Diskussionen in Lehrveranstaltungen
- Feedbackmethoden zur Lehrinnovation
- KI-gestütztes Schreiben vermitteln
